Mayor Mantello promises "ideas over ideology." This series documents how her ideology works.

Carmella names everything an operation. Operation Pothole. Operation Refresh. Project Sentinel. The military framing is not accidental. Operations have commanders. Commanders take credit. The troops disappear into the victory.

---

### [Part 2 — The General](/2026/07/28/the-general/)

Generals do not fill potholes. They name the operation, attend the press conference, and accept the credit. Carmella does not pave streets or replace lead pipes or write grant applications. The Department of Public Works does. The Comptroller does. Career city staff do. What she does is announce it.

---

### [Part 1 — The Mayor Announces](/2026/07/28/the-mayor-announces/)

"Mayor Carmella R. Mantello today announced..." leads every official City of Troy press release. Before you read it again, you should know what it is doing. Three moves, repeated across every category of city business, until the description of who governs Troy feels like the only description that has ever existed.
