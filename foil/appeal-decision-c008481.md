*Office of the Corporation Counsel — City of Troy, New York*
*July 28, 2026*

---

**Re: FOIL Appeal — Request No. C008481-071626 (DeVit)**

Dear Mr. DeVit:

I am in receipt of an appeal delivered on or about July 17, 2026. If you have occasion to appeal in the future, it would be exceedingly helpful if you furnished me a copy of your request and the response.

On July 16, 2026, you requested copies of written text, SMS, i-messages, and electronic messages between Alex Horton and media organizations, their employees, and contractors for the period January 1, 2026, to date of request. You specify that you are requesting these communications both from any city issued device and personal device used to conduct city business. Your request was duly acknowledged on July 16.

You received a further communication on July 16, 2026, advising that a diligent search of city records had returned no results relative to your request. You appeal on the grounds that: (1) the search was inadequate; (2) the scope of search was not disclosed; and (3) personal device records are subject to disclosure.

**Your appeal is denied.**

There is no evidence the search was inadequate or cursory. You requested certain of Mr. Horton's communications with news media. All he had to do was look at his phone(s) or other device(s) to determine if there were any records responsive to your request. It appears that he did so promptly and found none. Diligence requires nothing further.

As to your second ground of appeal, the law requires that the agency conduct a reasonably diligent search for the requested records. It does not require that every step be detailed to the requestor. In this case, however, Mr. Horton has represented to me that he searched his devices for responsive records and found none.

As to your third and last ground, Mr. Horton was well aware that your request sought records from his personal devices. He advises that the response to your request goes for those devices as well as city issued devices.

Very truly yours,

Richard T. Morrissey
City of Troy Acting Corporation Counsel
