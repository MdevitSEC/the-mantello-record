The Mantello Record is an independent publication based in Troy, New York.

Mayor Carmella Mantello won the 2023 mayoral race on a promise: "People over politics, ideas over ideology, competence over cronyism." This site exists to measure that promise against what actually happened.

Every entry here documents a decision, a failure, or an act of overreach by Mayor Mantello or someone in her administration. Sources are public meeting transcripts, local news coverage, and official city records. Everything is linked. Nothing is anonymous opinion dressed up as fact.

**What we cover:**

- Controversies involving Mayor Mantello and members of her administration
- Decisions by city bodies under her control or influence
- Failures of governance, transparency, and public accountability
- Actions that affect Troy residents but were made without their input

**What we are not:**

We have no affiliation with any political party, campaign, or elected official. We accept no advertising. No one funds this but us.

**Tips and sources:**

If you have information about the Mantello administration that belongs in the record, [submit it here](/submit-tip/). We review everything. We protect sources.
